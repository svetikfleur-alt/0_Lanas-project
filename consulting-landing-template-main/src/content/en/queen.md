# Queen
Status-led archetype for a woman who wants to project grounded authority, scale, and composed power.
## strengths
- Clear leadership signal
- Strong visual discipline
- Immediate sense of value
## blind spots
- Can feel too distant
- May underplay warmth and approachability
## visual recommendations
- Architectural tailoring and clean lines
- Dense textures, metal details, silk, and structured silhouettes
## business recommendations
- Emphasize premium offer framing
- Show decisions and leadership, not only inspiration
## prompt ideas
- Portrait in a bright architectural interior with depth
- Direct gaze with strong posture and composed gestures
