# Mentor
Archetype for a woman whose image is built on trust, depth, and the ability to guide others.
## strengths
- Warm sense of reliability
- Strong expert framing
- Calm and confident presence
## blind spots
- Can look overly safe
- May need more contrast to stand out
## visual recommendations
- Soft intellectual styling
- Natural fabrics, open gaze, and a calm palette
## business recommendations
- Package the method and client path
- Highlight case studies and transformation stories
